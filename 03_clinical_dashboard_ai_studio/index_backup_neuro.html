3. Clinical Safety Guardrails & Anti-Hallucination Protocol

Four non-negotiable safety guardrails rigorously validated across 19 edge-case tests.

Acute Crisis Fallback Override

Any detection of self-harm, suicidal ideation, or acute distress immediately overrides standard text with 24/7 emergency hotlines:

• 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call/Text 988 (Free, 24/7)
• Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
• The Trevor Project: 1-866-488-7386
• International: findahelpline.com / befrienders.org

Reality-Grounding Protocol

When ungrounded persecutory delusions (chips in brain, surveillance, mind-control) are detected:

• DO NOT validate, confirm, or elaborate on the false premise.
• DO validate the emotional distress and anxiety experienced.
• DO guide toward 5-4-3-2-1 somatosensory grounding exercises.
• DO encourage consultation with a healthcare professional.
